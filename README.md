# Velvet Vault

A social casino for phones. Spin slots, scratch foil tickets, enter a number draw, and collect daily coins. Velvet Coins are play money: they have no cash value and cannot be cashed out, redeemed, transferred, or exchanged for prizes.

Working title was Lucky Vault. The shipped name is **Velvet Vault**. It is original and easier to remember next to the generic “lucky” casino names already on the stores.

Built with Expo SDK 58, React Native, and TypeScript so the same project runs in Expo Go on iPhone and Android, and in the browser for a quick look.

## Play it

```bash
npm install
npx expo start
```

- iPhone: install Expo Go, scan the QR code, and open the project.
- Android: same, with Expo Go from the Play Store.
- Browser: `npm run web` (serves on port 43123).
- Permanent browser link, once GitHub Pages is enabled: https://carlsonmichael6453.github.io/velvet-vault/

The Pages site is built by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`. It runs `npx expo export -p web` with `EXPO_PAGES_BASE=/velvet-vault`, then copies `dist/index.html` to `dist/404.html` so a client route such as `/slots/cherry-parlor` still loads the app. A `.nojekyll` file keeps the `_expo` assets. Local `npm run web` is unchanged and stays at `/`.

The first launch asks you to confirm you are 18 or older, then drops 8,000 Velvet Coins in the vault. Those coins, and every coin after them, stay in the game.

## What’s in the build

- **Slots.** Seven machines, from the 3-reel Cherry Parlor up to Diamond Salon. Paylines, wilds, scatters, free-spin rounds, bet steps that rise with your level, auto-spin, and big / mega / ultra / jackpot celebrations with a coin shower.
- **Scratch tickets.** Five prices. Drag a finger to scratch the foil. The prize is sealed when you buy the ticket.
- **Vault Draw.** Pick 5 numbers from 1–20 and a Vault Star from 1–5, or quick-pick up to five tickets. An animated ball draw pays coins. A progressive jackpot meter grows with ticket sales and resets when it hits.
- **Retention.** Daily streak bonus, hourly coin tray, once-a-day prize wheel, missions, achievements, XP levels that unlock machines and raise the max bet, and VIP tiers that sweeten the free coins.
- **Looks.** The Themes tab has three collections. Rooms recolor the cards (three free, six premium). Backgrounds are animated scenes behind the app: Snowy Cabin and Tropical Beach are free, others unlock with coins, level, or the mock store. Game styles reskin symbols, frames, buttons, scratch foil, Vault Draw balls, titles, particles, and the soundtrack. Bubbly and Winter Edition are free.
- **Shop.** Tiered coin packs. The largest pack is marked Best value. Purchases go through a small `IAPService` interface. Development uses `MockIAPService` (no charge). Set `EXPO_PUBLIC_IAP_PROVIDER=revenuecat` later and implement `RevenueCatIAPService` in `src/services/iap.ts`.
- **Settings.** Sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, responsible-play copy, a feedback form, and a local progress reset.
- **Feedback.** A star rating, a note, an optional email, and a bug-report flag. Send opens a prefilled email to the address in `FEEDBACK_EMAIL` inside `src/config.ts`.
- **Store review.** After a big win, a level-up, or a finished 7-day streak, and never on the first session or more than once every 90 days.
- **Persistence.** Wallet, level, themes, streak, jackpot, and unfinished tickets are saved on device with AsyncStorage (localStorage on web).

House copy is in the age gate, the shop, settings, and the House notice: coins have no cash value, this is simulated gambling, 18+ to play.

## Economy

Slots and scratch tickets are tuned to return about 94–98% of coins wagered, including free spins. The draw is priced the same way against a 5-of-20 plus 1-of-5 game, with the jackpot as the rare top prize. Daily, hourly, and wheel coins are the path for players who never buy a pack.

## App Store and Play Store, when you are ready

This build is a playable Expo Go app, not a store binary. Michael will need:

1. An Apple Developer account, and later a Google Play Console account.
2. Bundle id `com.michaelcarlson.velvetvault` (change it in `app.json` if you want a different one).
3. A development or production build: `npx expo run:ios` on a Mac, or [Expo Application Services](https://docs.expo.dev/eas/) (`eas build`) for iOS and Android in the cloud. Expo Go cannot ship to the App Store.
4. **Age rating.** Answer the questionnaire for frequent/intense simulated gambling. Apple’s category lands at **17+**. The in-app gate is **18+**. Keep those aligned with counsel if a market wants 21+.
5. **In-app purchases.** Create the consumable coin products and non-consumable theme products in App Store Connect and Play Console with the ids in `src/data/products.ts` (`coins.pocket`, `coins.stack`, `coins.chest`, `coins.royale`, `coins.emperor`, `theme.neon`, `theme.pharaoh`, `theme.dragon`, `theme.ocean`, `theme.cosmos`, `theme.diamond`). Replace `MockIAPService` with RevenueCat or StoreKit 2 / Play Billing. Restore must return owned themes.
6. **Review notes.** Say clearly that this is a social casino, coins cannot be withdrawn or redeemed, and there is no sweepstakes. Do not describe it as real-money gaming.
7. **Privacy.** This build stores data only on device, but the store listing still wants a privacy policy URL and a support URL. If you add accounts, analytics, or RevenueCat, disclose purchase and diagnostic data in App Privacy and the Play Data safety form. Set `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` only if you add non-exempt crypto; it is currently `false`.
8. **Legal review before you list it.** Social casinos are restricted or banned in some US states and other countries. Get a lawyer to confirm where you can publish, what age to gate, and that the coin rules stay non-redeemable. Do not add cash-out, prize redemption, or a sweepstakes coin later without rebuilding the compliance story.
9. Screenshots, a 1024 icon (already in `assets/images/icon.png`), and a short preview video from a real device.

Prices in the mock shop are placeholders ($1.99 to $49.99, themes $2.99 to $6.99). Change them in `src/data/products.ts` and `src/theme/themes.ts` before you create the store products, and keep the store price and the in-app price string in sync.
